Session 79 — Into the Shadebarrow

🔍 The stone tree

The ring fits. The crypt is wrong.

Ki pressed Buddleia Austan's signet into the iron gate and it opened onto an unlit stair. What waits below is not the crypt Camellia remembers — no sarcophagi, no corridors, just a round chamber built around a tree carved from stone, and a scrawled tally of seven keys with six already struck through. A crystal on one branch turned a wall into a one-way portal. Through it: a room of blood-drinking vines held by wights and wraiths, and a fight that is not over.
